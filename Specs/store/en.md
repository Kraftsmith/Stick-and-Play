## Google Play (en-US)

**App name** (up to 30):

Stick & Play: Learn English

**Short description** (up to 80):

Collect stickers, learn English: words, letters and games for kids aged 4–6

**Full description** (up to 4000):

Your child plays to collect stickers – and every sticker is an English word.

Stick & Play is a learning game for children aged 4 to 6. Each short lesson earns coins, and coins buy new stickers for the child's albums. Along the way the child hears English words again and again: in the lesson, in the games and in the album.

No reading needed. Every instruction is spoken, every button is a picture. The English words are spoken by a native speaker with no translation, so the word goes straight to the picture.

WHAT YOUR CHILD LEARNS
• 64 English words in eight themes: farm, forest, sea, savanna, space and more
• 16 letters in phonics order: the sound, the name and the shape under a finger
• Letter teams like sh, ee and oy: two letters, one sound
• Listening: every word comes back dozens of times

GAMES
• Find the picture: the voice says a word, the child taps the picture
• Trace the letters: a finger follows the shape
• Puzzles from 4 to 9 pieces
• Pairs: a memory game where every card speaks English
• Race: the word shows which lane the coin is in
• Letter balls: balls of one colour line up into a word

FOR PARENTS
• No ads. Nothing to buy on the child's side; the parent area is behind a parental lock
• We collect no data about your child. Only a parent can have an account, and it is optional. The child's name stays on the device
• No way to lose: mistakes cost nothing and every lesson reaches the end
• Works offline: on a plane, in the car, at grandma's
• Daily time limit: 15, 20 or 30 minutes. Then the stickers go to sleep
• Up to 4 children, each with their own coins, stickers and albums
• Spoken instructions in English, German, French or Russian

Played in landscape, on tablets and phones.

Privacy policy: https://stickandplay.kids/privacy/
