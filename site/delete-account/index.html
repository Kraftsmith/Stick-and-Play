Delete your Stick & Play account

Stick & Play by ShipAlly Studio

An account in Stick & Play is optional. It belongs to a parent and keeps a copy of the children’s game progress. You can delete it at any time.

In the app

  1. On the main screen, tap the lock For Parents at the bottom left.
  2. Tap the numbers in the order shown.
  3. Open Settings. At the top is the Account block.
  4. Tap Delete account, type the word DELETE and confirm.

The same steps work in the browser game at stickandplay.kids/play.

By e-mail

If you can no longer open the app, write to yauhenisorokin@gmail.com from the e-mail address of the account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • the parent’s e-mail address and password;
  • the game progress of every child profile stored in the account;
  • all sign-in sessions with their IP addresses and device types;
  • redeemed promo codes and the premium status given by a promo code.

It is removed from the database at once, and from the backups within 7 days.

What is not deleted

  • The progress saved on the device itself stays there, so the child can keep playing. To remove it, uninstall the app (in the browser: clear the site’s data).
  • A subscription bought in Google Play is not cancelled by deleting the account. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Deleting only one child’s progress

In For Parents → Settings, the Children block can delete one child profile. Its copy in the account is kept for 30 days in case it was deleted by mistake, then removed. Write to us if you want it removed sooner.
