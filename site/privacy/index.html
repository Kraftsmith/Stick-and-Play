Stick&Play

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

In short

  • Stick & Play has no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
  • We do not collect personal information from children. The child’s name, if you type one, never leaves the device.
  • The app works fully without an account. An account is optional, for a parent who wants to keep the progress safe and use it on several devices. It holds the parent’s e-mail address and the game progress.
  • The account and everything in it can be deleted at any time, in the app or by e-mail.

1. Who we are

Stick & Play is an English learning game for children aged 4–6, made by ShipAlly Studio (“we”, “us”). It is available as an app on Google Play and as a game in the browser at stickandplay.kids/play. We are responsible for the data described here.

Contact for anything about privacy: yauhenisorokin@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on the device

To let the child play, the app saves on the device itself:

  • the game progress: coins, stickers and albums, completed lessons and their stars, which words the child already knows;
  • settings: the language of the spoken instructions, sound volumes, the daily time limit and today’s playing time;
  • the child’s name or nickname, if the parent types one (it is optional), and the picture chosen for the child’s profile from the app’s own set of pictures.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the app (or clear the site’s data in the browser). The parent can also save it to a file and load it on another device; that file stays wherever you put it.

3. The optional parent account

Only a grown-up can create an account: it is inside the parent area, behind a parental lock. Children cannot reach it. If a parent signs in, we store:

WhatWhy
The parent’s e-mail addressTo sign in, and to send a code when the password is forgotten. We do not send newsletters or marketing.
The password, as a one-way hashTo sign in. We never see or store the password itself.
The game progress of each child profile: coins, stickers, albums, completed lessons, known words, game settings, the profile picture from the app’s set, and how the app calls the grown-up (for example “Mum”)To restore the progress after reinstalling and to show the same progress on every device of the family. The child’s name is not included.
A random device identifier made by the appTo tell the family’s devices apart when merging progress. It is not a serial number or an advertising ID.
For each signed-in session: the IP address and the device or browser typeSecurity: to protect the account against misuse. Kept until the session ends.
Promo codes the parent redeemed and the premium statusTo open the premium content for the family.

The legal basis is the performance of the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and, for the session security data, our legitimate interest in keeping accounts safe (Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. Children’s privacy

The app is made for young children and is built so that it does not need their personal information. We do not ask for or store a child’s name, age, birthday, photo, voice, location or contacts on our servers. The app does not use the microphone, camera or location. It has no chat, no social features and no links out that a child can follow without passing the parental lock. There are no ads and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. This is how we meet the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the USA, the GDPR rules for children in the EU and the Google Play Families policy.

5. Who processes the data for us

We do not sell or share data. These services store or handle it for us, only to run Stick & Play:

  • Vercel — hosting of the website and the server (region: Frankfurt, Germany);
  • Neon — the database with accounts and progress (region: Frankfurt, Germany);
  • Resend — sends the password reset code; receives only the e-mail address;
  • Google Play — delivers the app. When in-app purchases are added, payments will be handled by Google Play under Google’s privacy policy; we never see card details.

The data is stored in the European Union. Some of these companies are based in the USA; transfers are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data — until the account is deleted.
  • A deleted child profile — 30 days, so that it can be restored on request; then removed.
  • Database backups — at most 7 days. After an account is deleted, its data disappears from the backups within 7 days.
  • Technical request logs of the hosting (IP address, time, the page or request) — a few days at most, for security.

7. Deleting the account and your rights

To delete the account and all progress stored with it: in the app, open For Parents, then Settings → Account → Delete account. Or write to us from the account’s e-mail address. Details: Delete your account.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data (we send the progress as a JSON file), to correct it, to restrict or object to its use. Write to yauhenisorokin@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. In the EU you can complain to your data protection authority.

8. The website

The website and the browser game set no advertising or analytics cookies. The browser game keeps the progress in the browser’s local storage; a signed-in parent gets one cookie needed to stay signed in. The home page currently loads the Nunito font from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers.

9. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as hashes, sign-in attempts are limited, and the database can be reached only by our server.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect, we will say so in the app before it applies.
